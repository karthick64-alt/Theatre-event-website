Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: December 1, 2024

General Policy

All ticket sales are final. However, we understand that circumstances may change, and we offer refunds and exchanges in specific situations as outlined below.

Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be available in the following circumstances:

  • Show Cancellation: Full refund if we cancel a performance
  • Date Change: Refund available if show date changes significantly
  • Medical Emergency: Refund may be granted with documentation
  • 48+ Hours Notice: Exchange or credit may be available

Exchange Policy

Ticket exchanges are subject to availability and may be available up to 48 hours before the performance:

  • Exchanges must be for the same show or equal value
  • A $5 exchange fee may apply
  • Exchanges are subject to seat availability

Processing Time

Refunds, when approved, will be processed within 7-10 business days. The refund will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase.

Non-Refundable Items

The following are non-refundable:

  • Service fees
  • Processing fees
  • Gift shop purchases
  • Concession items

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us:

  • Email: refunds@qproductions.com
  • Phone: (555) 123-4567
  • Include your booking reference number and reason for refund

If you have any questions about our refund policy, please contact us at refunds@qproductions.com
